Sloth

A sloth is a slow-moving mammal that lives in the tropical forests of Central and South America and hangs from tree branches, feeding mainly on leaves. Sloths are also known for very low metabolism and deliberate movements.

  • The English word “sloth” comes from an old term for slowness.
  • The sloth coat hosts algae, fungi, mites, beetles, and moths in a mini-ecosystem.
  • Sloths remember routes through their personal network of trees
  • A sloth can hold its breath underwater for up to 40 minutes.
